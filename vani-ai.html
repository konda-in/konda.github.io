What is Vani AI?

Vani AI is Konda Academy's conversational AI tutor. It uses voice interaction to help students practice, ask questions, and get explanations in a natural, spoken format — closer to a real tutoring session than a static app.

What is Vani Web?

Vani Web is the web application that brings Vani AI to students, live at vaniai.app. It supports exam preparation workflows including practice sessions, doubt-solving, and progress tracking.
